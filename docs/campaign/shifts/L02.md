# L02: Hello, World Roast

Act I: Query · 3 tables · 1 seed

The cutscene _The Scrapyard_ plays before this shift: Too many tickets for one pair of hands, and the robot Moka threw out.

Query is awake and ready to say hello to the world. The very first guest would like a coffee. No pressure.

**Goal.** A customer wants a coffee, but the kitchen can only make drinks from a written ticket handed over from the counter.

**Lesson.** Wait for Orders, Take up a sheet from the paper stack, and Write Coffee on it. Move right 1, Deposit right at the kitchen handoff, then Move left 1 back to the register. Checkout is automatic.

**Stars.** One for serving every seed, two for 8 blocks or fewer, three for also finishing in 7 executed steps or fewer. The reference uses 6 blocks.

## Customers in the first seed

- “coffee”

## Reference programs

Written in the text-editor language. Moka runs the kitchen and Pip the floor until a robot takes the role over.

```text
LISTEN
```

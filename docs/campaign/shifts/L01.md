# L01: Brew Beginnings

Prologue · 2 tables · 1 seed

The cutscene _The Keys_ plays before this shift: Aunt Lou’s last card brings Niko her café, seven months late.

The doors are open again! Niko greets the very first customer, Moka runs the kitchen and Pip carries the drinks. Everyone is very busy. Mostly Niko.

**Goal.** Watch how one customer’s order goes from the register to a finished drink and a cleared table. Niko writes every ticket by hand: spot the job Query could take over.

**Lesson.** Niko: The café is ours now. Watch a customer order, receive a drink and leave a clean table. Follow each order from the register, through the kitchen, to the table.

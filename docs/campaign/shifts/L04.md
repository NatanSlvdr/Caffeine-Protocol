# L04: Groundhog Latte

Act I: Query · 5 tables · 3 seeds

One happy customer is a good start. But another guest is already at the door. And another. And another…

**Goal.** Several customers are waiting, but Query stops after the first order. Every customer in the queue needs to be served.

**Lesson.** Put a Jump destination above Wait for Orders, and a Jump back to it after returning to the register. Keep serving every customer.

**Stars.** One for serving every seed, two for 14 blocks or fewer, three for also finishing in 136 executed steps or fewer. The reference uses 12 blocks.

## Customers in the first seed

- “coffee”
- “tea”
- “tea please”
- “coffee”

## Reference programs

Written in the text-editor language. Moka runs the kitchen and Pip the floor until a robot takes the role over.

### Query (counter)

```text
POSITION listen
LISTEN
TAKE UP
IF tea IN CUSTOMER SPEECH
  ITEM tea
ELSE
  ITEM coffee
END
MOVE RIGHT 1
DEPOSIT RIGHT
MOVE LEFT 1
JUMP listen
```
